What makes a supplier
the right partner?

Production & Commercial

  • Relevant category capability and OEM / ODM experience
  • Transparent costing and competitive pricing
  • Capacity, minimums and order flexibility
  • Sampling speed and production calendar discipline
  • Export experience and long-term reliability

Quality & Compliance

  • Documented quality-control process and inspection access
  • Material specifications and batch traceability
  • Verifiable certifications and supplier documentation
  • Clear corrective-action process
  • Accurate shipment, packing and export documentation
