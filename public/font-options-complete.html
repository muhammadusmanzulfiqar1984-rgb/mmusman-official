My Recommendation

Option 3 (Cormorant + Inter with Bold Contrast) — You don't need new fonts. Just:

  1. Headings: Change weight from 300 → 500
  2. Body: Change weight from 300 → 400, add letter-spacing +0.4px
  3. Labels: Keep DM Mono but increase weight to 500

Why this works: Weight contrast makes thin fonts feel intentional. Tighter spacing gives optical density against your dark background.

If you want a fresh font stack: Option 4 (Plus Jakarta Sans) or Option 6 (Work Sans) — both are sharp, professional, and don't need heavy tuning.

✓ Quickest fix: Option 3 (no new fonts needed)
