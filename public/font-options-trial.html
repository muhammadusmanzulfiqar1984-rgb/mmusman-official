Why fonts feel dull:

1. Weight mismatch: If headings are too light (300) and body is also light (300), there's no visual hierarchy — everything reads flat.

2. Loose letter-spacing: Thin fonts need tighter spacing to feel premium. Loose spacing makes them feel airy/weak.

3. Low contrast with background: Gold (#d4c49a) on very dark (#080808) reads dull without strong weight separation.

4. Font choice: Some faces (like thin Garamond) are beautiful but fragile in small sizes on screens.

Solutions:

✓ Use weight contrast (300 headings, 400+ body)

✓ Add letter-spacing to thin text (+0.3–0.5px)

✓ Increase heading sizes so thin strokes read as intentional

✓ Consider small-caps or text-transform: uppercase for labels

Recommendation: Option 4 (Bold Contrast)
